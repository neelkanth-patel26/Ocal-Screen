2. PRIVACY POLICY & DATA PROTECTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ocal Screen is engineered with a strict Privacy-First Architecture. Your data
privacy is guaranteed under the following terms:

A. 100% LOCAL & ON-DEVICE PROCESSING
   - All video recordings, screen captures, audio streams (microphone and 
     system audio), and cursor telemetry are processed 100% locally on your 
     device. No video or audio data is ever transmitted, uploaded, or analyzed 
     on external servers or cloud services.

B. OFFLINE AI CAPTIONING
   - Automatic voiceover captioning operates strictly offline using on-device 
     neural networks. Your voice recordings remain private and on your device 
     at all times.

C. DIAGNOSTICS & BUG REPORTING
   - Ocal Screen does NOT collect background telemetry or analytics.
   - When submitting a bug report via the in-app "Report an Issue" modal, 
     basic environment details (CPU model, RAM size, GPU renderer, Windows 
     version) are formatted locally for inclusion in your GitHub issue post. 
     You can toggle or inspect this information prior to submission.

D. NO USER TRACKING OR PROFILING
   - Ocal Screen contains zero user tracking code, zero advertising pixels, 
     and zero third-party behavioral profiling SDKs.
