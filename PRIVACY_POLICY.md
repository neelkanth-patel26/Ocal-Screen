## 1. Introduction

Ocal Software ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. **Ocal Screen** is an open-source, desktop screen recorder and video editor designed with a strict **Privacy-First Architecture**. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when using Ocal Screen.

---

## 2. 100% Local Processing & Zero Data Collection

- **No Remote Telemetry**: Ocal Screen does not collect, store, track, or transmit your personal data, screen recordings, voice recordings, or usage metrics to any remote server or third-party service.
- **Offline Operation**: All core features — including screen capture, webcam overlay, microphone/system audio recording, AI auto-zoom generation, and speech-to-text auto-captions — run **100% locally on your computer**.
- **No Cloud Uploads**: Your recorded videos, audio files, and exported MP4/GIF outputs remain strictly on your local disk.

---

## 3. Permissions Requested

Ocal Screen requests operating system permissions strictly necessary to fulfill your recording commands:
- **Screen Capture / Recording**: Required to capture your desktop display or individual application windows.
- **Microphone Access**: Required if you choose to include voice commentary in your recordings.
- **Camera Access**: Required if you enable the webcam Picture-in-Picture overlay.
- **Accessibility / Cursor Access**: Required on macOS and Windows to record cursor position telemetry for AI auto-zoom positioning.

---

## 4. Local Preferences & Storage

Ocal Screen stores user preferences (such as selected studio theme, accent color, default cursor size, and HUD layout) locally on your machine in configuration files. You may clear or reset these preferences at any time by deleting the application configuration directory.
